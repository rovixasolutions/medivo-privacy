1. Introduction

Rovixa Solutions (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Medivo mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

By using Medivo, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Who We Are

Medivo is a medicine and pill reminder app that helps you schedule doses, receive reminders, and track whether you took your medicine. It is developed by Rovixa Solutions.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information you enter in the app (stored on your device)

Medivo stores the following data locally on your phone (using an on-device database and app settings):

  • Medicine names and related details you add
  • Dose schedules and reminder times
  • Dose history (taken / skipped / missed, as recorded in the app)
  • App preferences (for example language, premium status flags)
  • Onboarding / permission-related preferences

We do not require you to create an account.
We do not upload your medicine list or dose history to our own servers.

3.2 Purchase information (Google Play)

If you buy Premium (monthly, yearly, or lifetime), payments are processed by Google Play. We receive purchase status needed to unlock Premium features (for example ads-free access). We do not receive your full payment card details.

3.3 Automatically collected technical / advertising / analytics data

Medivo uses third-party services that may collect device and usage information:

  • Google AdMob (ads for free users): May collect advertising ID, device information, IP address, and interaction data to show ads. Premium users do not see ads.
  • Firebase Analytics: App usage events (for example screen views) to understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reports and technical diagnostics to fix bugs and improve stability.

These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google’s privacy policies.

4. Permissions We Request

Medivo may ask for these Android permissions:

Permission Why
Notifications To send medicine dose reminders
Exact alarms / scheduled alarms So reminders can fire on time
Internet / network state Ads, analytics, crash reporting, and Play Billing
Boot completed To reschedule reminders after device restart
Vibrate Reminder alerts
Battery optimization exception (optional) To improve reminder reliability on some devices

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Some features (especially reminders) may not work correctly without required permissions.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide medicine reminders and dose tracking
  • Remember your schedules and preferences on your device
  • Process Premium purchases through Google Play
  • Show ads to free users (via AdMob)
  • Measure app usage and improve features (Analytics)
  • Diagnose crashes and improve reliability (Crashlytics)
  • Respond to support requests you send us by email

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with:

  • Google Play – for in-app purchases / subscriptions
  • Google AdMob – for advertising (free users)
  • Firebase (Google) – for analytics and crash reporting
  • Authorities – only if required by law

Your medicine and reminder data stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example screenshots or email).

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Core health-related reminder data is stored locally on your device
  • We use industry-standard third-party SDKs (Google) for ads, analytics, billing, and crash reporting
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the app

If you uninstall Medivo or clear app data, local medicine/reminder data may be permanently deleted from that device.

8. Children’s Privacy

Medivo is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Medical Disclaimer

Medivo is a reminder and tracking tool only. It is not a medical device, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow your doctor’s or pharmacist’s instructions. Do not rely on the app as your only reminder method for critical medication.

10. Premium, Ads, and Your Choices

  • Free users may see advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Premium users get an ads-free experience (subject to active purchase/subscription)
  • You can manage advertising preferences through your device settings (for example Reset Advertising ID / Ad privacy settings)
  • You can disable analytics/ads-related services at the device or Google account level where available

11. International Users / EEA-UK Consent

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, advertising partners may require consent for personalized ads under applicable privacy laws. Where required, consent may be collected through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or similar consent tools before personalized ads are shown.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Because most Medivo reminder data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. For requests related to ads/analytics/purchases handled by Google, you may also need to use Google’s tools or contact us so we can help where possible.
